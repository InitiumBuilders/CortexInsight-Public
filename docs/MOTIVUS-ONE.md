# Motivus.One: the subscription tier (proposal)

This is a proposal for the founder to ratify. Nothing in it is wired into billing yet; the app carries the seams (the About panel names the service, Settings → Integrations holds the keys, the gate is per operator) and nothing else. The line the app already shows, verbatim: **This Is A Service From Motivus.One**.

## What the free tree gives everyone

Everything that runs on the operator's own machine and their own Claude Code subscription: the console, the board with its flow and its close, the loops with their contract and their scores, workflows with gates, Motus Max with the pre-read and the seat clock, the rhythm and the reading, the Davara view, the open-source gate. No account, no key, no phone-home. The fleet bridge, the brief and the agent inbox all stay local.

## What a Motivus.One subscription could add

Only things that are genuinely services, so the free tree never becomes crippled software:

1. **Her evolving mind, streamed.** The Davara baseline is a living canon that updates on its own version bumps. Operators on the tier receive the stream (davara.dev already serves `baseline.json` this way) and the covenant loop that evolves it; the free tree reads whatever clone the operator has.
2. **The second stack, hosted.** Operators who do not want to hold an OpenAI key could route the GPT seat and the studio through a Motivus.One key with a monthly image and turn allowance. The app already keys everything by `settings.openaiKeyEnc`; a hosted key is the same door with a different owner.
3. **Sync between machines.** The vault is one machine's. A tier could carry the board, the loops, the receipts and the learnings between an operator's machines through the managed hub that Davara.DEV already runs for MCP state.
4. **The commons.** Work units to the compute pool, verified and paid, already exist behind the board's "To the pool"; the tier is the membership that makes a pool node count.

## What must stay true whatever the tier

- The free tree runs whole without a Motivus.One account. A missing tier is an absence of services, never a lock on the operator's own data.
- Keys and vaults stay on the operator's machine, sealed by the OS keystore.
- Nothing in the app phones home unless the operator turned a service on. The open-source gate and this document are the promise.

## Pricing

The founder's call. The ladder used elsewhere (Operator $50, MAX $200, DUO $22 pooled) is the obvious frame; this tool is an operator's instrument, so the Operator rung is the natural home.

## What the app needs before any of this is sold

- A `motivus.one` license endpoint and a sealed license key in Settings, verified the way the other keys are.
- The stream and the hub reachable from the app (both exist on Davara.DEV today).
- The open-source gate at zero warnings, and a first public release people can run without the founder's machine.
